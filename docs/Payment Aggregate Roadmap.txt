Payment Lifecycle

The aggregate will enforce the following business flow:

Create Payment
       │
       ▼
Pending
       │
       ▼
Authorize
       │
       ▼
Authorized
       │
       ▼
Capture
       │
       ▼
Captured
       │
       ▼
Settled


Alternative flows:


Pending
   │
   ▼
Cancelled

--

Authorized
   │
   ▼
Failed

--

Captured
   │
   ▼
Refunded
