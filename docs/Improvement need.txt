One More Enterprise Improvement

Since we're building this as a Senior/Lead-level project, I'd also suggest replacing repetitive if validations in the factory with reusable guard methods once we introduce a Guard utility class.

For example:

Guard.Against.EmptyGuid(inventoryId, nameof(inventoryId));

Guard.Against.EmptyGuid(productId, nameof(productId));

Guard.Against.EmptyGuid(warehouseId, nameof(warehouseId));

Guard.Against.Negative(reorderLevel, nameof(reorderLevel));

Guard.Against.ZeroOrNegative(maximumQuantity, nameof(maximumQuantity));

This makes factory methods across all aggregates (Order, Customer, Product, Inventory, Payment) much shorter and more consistent.
